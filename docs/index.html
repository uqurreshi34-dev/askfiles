Features

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

🤖
AI File Search
Ask in plain English — "find my bank statements" or "show my largest videos". AI understands your files.
🔍
Smart Search
Search inside your documents privately. Everything happens on your device — no internet, no servers.
🛡️
Secure Vault
Lock sensitive files behind PIN and biometrics. Hidden from your gallery and other apps.
📊
Storage Breakdown
See exactly what's using your storage — images, videos, documents, downloads. Clear and instant.
🗂️
Duplicate Finder
Find and remove duplicate files taking up space. Smart matching by name and size.
☁️
Cloud Backup & Settings Sync
Back up vault files and sync your tags, favourites and pinned folders across devices via Google Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox.
🏷️
Tags
Organise files across folders without moving them. Colour-coded tags appear on your home screen — tap to see all tagged files instantly. Sync across devices with Pro.
🌙
Dark Mode
Full dark mode support across every screen. Easy on the eyes, day or night.
🔒
App Lock
Protect the entire app with PIN or biometrics. Your files stay private.
🖥️
Network (SMB, SFTP, FTP)
Browse your PC, Mac or NAS over WiFi. Download and upload files — credentials encrypted on-device.
📶
WiFi Transfer
Move files between any device on your WiFi through a browser. No cables, no apps, zero setup.
📄
PDF Tools
Create PDFs from photos, extract pages as images, and merge multiple PDFs — all on your device.
🔄
Image Converter
Convert photos between JPG, PNG and WEBP — fully offline, no upload needed. Supports HEIC on Android 10+.
